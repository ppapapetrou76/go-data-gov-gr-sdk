How it counts

A landed PR is credited once to its author, replacing the commits, rebases and replies behind it. A review counts once per reviewer per PR, on someone else's PR. Merges go to the person who merged, or to the person whose merge command a bot acted on. Authoring weight sums the size weights of a person's landed PRs; reviewing weight sums the weights of the PRs they reviewed, multiplied by when the review left feedback.
